Estimated delivery times exclude weekends and may not account for public holidays or government delays. No result, approval or completion date is guaranteed unless expressly agreed in writing.

6. Professionals

Some matters require licensed attorneys, notaries, translators or local professionals. Galiel may coordinate these services or refer the matter where appropriate.

The client should protect their account credentials. Submitted files may contain confidential or sensitive information and will be used for assessing or providing the requested service subject to the published Privacy Policy.

8. Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Galiel does not store card numbers or CVV. Successful payment starts review, not government approval. Payment receipts are distinct from tax invoices, which are issued as applicable.
